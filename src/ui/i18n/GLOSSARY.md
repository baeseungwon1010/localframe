# Korean glossary

One Korean word per English term, everywhere in the interface. Labels are short nouns; messages,
hints and errors are polite, plain 해요체 sentences ("파일을 저장했어요", "레이어를 먼저 선택하세요").
Keep product words people know from design tools in their usual Korean form.
